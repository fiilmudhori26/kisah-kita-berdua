# Design

## Direction
Warm, calm, minimal, intimate. The product should feel like a personal keepsake rather than a social network.

## Initial tokens
- Background: warm ivory
- Surface: white
- Text: deep warm charcoal
- Accent: muted rose
- Borders: soft warm gray
- Radius: rounded-xl / rounded-2xl

## UX constraints
- Mobile-first layout
- Clear empty states
- Minimal navigation
- No public profiles, follower counts, or social feed mechanics
